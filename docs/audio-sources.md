# Guitar recordings

The six open-string recordings in `public/guitar/` come from [Meta Steel Guitar](https://github.com/facebookincubator/Meta-open-sound/tree/main/systems/instruments/meta-steel-guitar), velocity layer 2. That collection is released under [CC0 1.0](https://github.com/facebookincubator/Meta-open-sound/blob/main/systems/instruments/meta-steel-guitar/LICENSE).

Each source OGG was trimmed to 3.2 seconds, faded over the final 0.5 seconds, mixed to mono, and encoded as MP3. Tone.js shifts these recordings by up to three semitones for the app's first-position fretted notes.
